Make a tier list for anything

Tier Ranker is a free tier list maker for ranking whatever you're arguing about: video game characters, snacks, movies, albums, your friends' cooking. No account, no downloads, and it works on your phone.

  1. Add items. Type text, upload or paste images, add an image link, or search for pictures without leaving the page.
  2. Rank them. Drag each item into a tier. Drag again to change your mind, or onto the trash to remove it.
  3. Make it yours. Rename the list and its rows, pick row colors, and add, reorder or delete tiers.

FAQ

Is Tier Ranker free?
Yes. It's free, needs no account, and runs entirely in your browser.
Can I use my own pictures?
Yes. Upload images, paste them from your clipboard, drop them onto the page, add them by link, or search Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons right inside the app.
How many tiers can I have?
Up to 10 rows. The default is S, A, B, C, D and 💩, and you can rename, recolor, reorder or delete any of them.
Where is my tier list saved?
In your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and your list is still there when you come back on the same device.
